Common questions

Valeting in Felpham, answered

Do you come to my home in Felpham?

Yes — Echelon is fully mobile. We come to your driveway or workplace anywhere in Felpham (PO22) and the surrounding area, and bring our own water and power. You don't need to be in, as long as we can reach the car.

How much is a valet in Felpham?

Prices start at £52 for an Express valet, £72 for our most-booked Standard, and £125 for a Deep Interior. You get a fixed price when you book — no surprises on the day.

How long will it take?

An Express is about 45 minutes, a Standard around 90, and a Deep Interior up to three hours. We give you a slot when you book so you can plan your day.

What if it rains?

Light rain is usually fine for the interior and protective work. For a full exterior detail we'll move your slot to the next dry window — we'll message you rather than turn up and do half a job.
